STREET FIGHTER 2010 - V9 TRANSLATION NOTES

Principles
- Japanese game script remains the primary source.
- The Japanese manual is used as canonical terminology/context, not as license to add story material absent from the game.
- Natural English is preferred when it does not change meaning.
- Original utterance boundaries, quote controls, pauses, and renderer scrolling behavior are preserved.

Manual-confirmed terminology
- Parasite
- Armored Insect
- Galaxy Police
- Open Power
- Dimensional Door
- Flip Shield

Key prose revisions
- 'made by' -> 'built by the Galaxy Police' in the Ballantine-type reveal.
- Dagobah instruction now reads 'you'll be one step closer to perfection,' matching the Japanese wording and Jose's perfection motif.
- Kevin's pain dialogue consistently retains 'pain' as a repeated story motif.
- Jose's pore/body explanation is rendered as a concrete biological invasion rather than vague armor language.
- Final mission briefing uses 'exterminate,' 'Open Power,' and 'Dimensional Door' in line with the Japanese text/manual.
- 'Me... a Parasite...?' restores the clipped shock of Japanese 'ore... parasite...?' rather than expanding it into a full sentence.

Unresolved proper noun
- BALLANTINE-TYPE remains the best-supported reading of バランタイン型. The manual does not supply a Latin spelling.
